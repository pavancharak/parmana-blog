This is part 8 of the series Compliance in practice. Part 7 covered keeping evidence inside your network. This part covers how a person's approval shows up in that evidence.

An approval is recorded with the action it covers. An approval expires and can be used only once, so an auditor can check that it was valid when the action ran.

The approval concept page sets out the rule and how it is enforced.

## What it does not do

The record shows an approval was given and used. It does not show that the approver understood the action, which is a question for your own process.
