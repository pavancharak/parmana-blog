This is part 9 of the series Compliance in practice. Part 8 covered approvals on the record. This part turns the earlier parts into a list of audit questions.

Ask what was requested, who approved it, which rule decided it, and what ran. The trust record answers the first three, and the execution record answers the last.

Ask whether the evidence is intact. The verification checks answer that.

## What it does not do

A list of questions is a starting point, not an audit. The audit scope is set by your auditor and your own policies.
