This is part 2 of the series Product in practice. Part 1 let you see the product run. This part helps you choose where to read next.

The choose-your-path table has one row for each goal. Each row names the place to start, what you need first, and the first command or request.

If two rows seem to apply, the table says to do the earlier one first.

## What it does not do

The table routes you to a starting point. It does not cover every path, and the pages it links to hold the detail.
