This is part 10, the last part of Compliance in practice. Part 9 turned the parts into audit questions. This part connects those questions to the regulation mapping.

The regulation mapping sets out the provisions a compliance team cites, and what Parmana provides for each. The evidence in this series answers the questions those provisions raise.

Use the mapping to decide which evidence to show, and the trust record to show it.

## What it does not do

The mapping is not legal advice. Your legal team decides whether the evidence meets a provision.
