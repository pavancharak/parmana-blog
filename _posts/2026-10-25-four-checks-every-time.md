This is part 2 of the series Compliance in practice. Part 1 described what a trust record holds. This part explains how that record is verified.

Verification always runs four checks, and each result is reported independently. A record can pass one check and fail another, and the report shows which.

Because the checks are reported separately, a reviewer can see exactly which part of the evidence is in doubt.

## What it does not do

Four passing checks do not prove the decision was the right one. They show the record is intact and signed, which is a narrower claim.
