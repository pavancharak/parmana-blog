This is part 4 of the series Product in practice. Part 3 covered self-hosting. This part places Parmana beside the tools a team may already have.

Identity and access tools decide who may call an API. API gateways decide whether a request may pass, and how fast. Guardrails decide whether text looks harmful.

Parmana decides whether one specific action, with its exact parameters, may run now, requires a person's signed approval, and signs a record of what ran.

## What it does not do

The comparison page says where Parmana is weaker. Read that section before you decide that Parmana replaces another tool.
