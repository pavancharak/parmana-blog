This is part 5 of the series Product in practice. Part 4 compared Parmana with other tools. This part shows one connector in use, the HubSpot connector.

The HubSpot integration page describes the actions the connector can perform once a request is approved. Each action still goes through the same checks as any other.

The connector is one of the built-in connectors. It is not the only way to connect a business system.

## What it does not do

The page covers the actions it lists. An action not on the list is not supported by this connector.
