This is part 8 of the series Operations in practice. Part 7 covered confirming a dependency fix. This part covers the record that holds the team's answers about security and licensing.

The advisory record is dated. It says what changed, what each answer rests on, what is still open, and why. It does not replace the reference that describes what is built.

Update it in the same change that alters an answer, so the record and the code do not drift apart.

## What it does not do

The record describes the position on the date it was written. Check the date before you rely on any answer.
