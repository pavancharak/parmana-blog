Part 3 of AI governance in practice. Part 2 covered policies as versioned files. This part covers who may change a policy, and who must approve the change.

A policy change follows a maker and checker process. One person proposes the change, and a different person approves it. A policy that has not been approved through this process cannot authorize anything.

The governance guide describes the steps, from proposal to approval, and how the approved change becomes part of the public record in the next part.

## What it does not do

The guide describes the process. It does not say who your approvers should be, and it does not replace your own change control.

## How we checked it

The steps are described in the policy governance guide, linked from this post.

## What is still open

The guide must match the code. If a step changes, the guide changes in the same pull request.
