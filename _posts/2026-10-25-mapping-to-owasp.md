Part 10, the last part of AI governance in practice. Part 9 mapped Parmana to regulation. This part maps it to the security risks that AI applications face.

The OWASP risk lists for large language model applications and for agentic applications describe the risks an AI system faces. The OWASP mapping shows how Parmana addresses each one it covers.

The mapping lets a security team check the controls against a list it already uses.

## What it does not do

The mapping does not cover every item on either list, and it does not say that a covered item is fully addressed. Read the page for the items it leaves out.

## How we checked it

The mapping is in the public documentation, next to the threat model it relates to.
