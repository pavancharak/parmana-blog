Part 9 of AI governance in practice. Part 8 covered telling approvers when their signature is needed. This part maps those controls to the rules compliance teams cite.

Compliance teams ask how an AI agent is overseen and how its actions are recorded. The regulation mapping sets out the provisions they cite, including the EU AI Act's articles on record keeping and human oversight, the NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the Reserve Bank of India's guidance.

Each provision is mapped to what Parmana provides for it.

## What it does not do

The mapping is not legal advice, and it does not say that a provision is met. Read each row with its gaps, and ask your legal team to confirm the position.

## How we checked it

The mapping is in the public documentation, and it names each provision it covers.
