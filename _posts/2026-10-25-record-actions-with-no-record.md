This is part 4 of the series Compliance in practice. Part 3 covered where records are kept. This part covers the gap that remains when a record is missing.

The execution intent is written before an action is released. If an action was released without its record, the intent is the place to start looking.

The intent page describes how to find those cases and how to repair them.

## What it does not do

The repair does not undo the action that already ran. It restores the evidence so the case can be reviewed.
