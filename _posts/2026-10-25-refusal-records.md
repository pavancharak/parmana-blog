Part 7 of AI governance in practice. Part 6 covered the facts that justify an action. This part covers the evidence left when an action is refused.

An approved execution always leaves signed evidence behind. A rejected request should leave evidence too, and the refusal record is that evidence.

The record is signed and can be verified independently of Parmana's servers.

## What it does not do

The refusal page sets out two scope caveats that bound the claim. Read them before you describe a refusal to anyone outside your team.

## How we checked it

The builder and the signing code for refusal records are named in the page.

## What is still open

Refusal records only cover requests that reach the decision. A request that fails earlier leaves its own, different evidence.
