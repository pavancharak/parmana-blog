This is part 6 of the series Operations in practice. Part 5 described what runs in production. This part shows how to diagnose a problem with it.

The production troubleshooting runbook is built from real incidents and fixes. Each entry gives the symptom you see, why it happens, how to check it, and the fix.

Start from the symptom in the table, then follow the check before you apply the fix.

## What it does not do

The runbook covers the problems the team has already seen. A new problem needs a new entry, written from its real cause.
