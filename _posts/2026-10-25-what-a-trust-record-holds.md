This is part 1 of the series Compliance in practice. It explains the central evidence document, which later parts rely on.

An execution trust record is the canonical, signed record of everything Parmana knows about one business transaction. It is immutable, so a change to it is visible.

A reviewer can read what was requested, what was decided, and what was executed, from the record alone.

## What it does not do

The record shows what Parmana knows. It does not show facts Parmana never received, such as what a connected system did outside Parmana.
