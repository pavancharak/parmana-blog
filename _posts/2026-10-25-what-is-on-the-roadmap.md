This is part 8 of the series Product in practice. Part 7 covered the SDKs that exist today. This part covers what is planned and does not ship yet.

Every item on the roadmap is marked as not yet shipped, unless a point says otherwise. None of it ships in the current server by default.

Read the roadmap as a list of intentions, and check the claims register for what is actually built.

## What it does not do

A roadmap item is not a promise of a date. It can change, or be dropped.
