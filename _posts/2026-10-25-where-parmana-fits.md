This is part 10, the last part of Product in practice. Part 9 answered common questions. This part sums up where Parmana fits, using the earlier parts of the series.

Parmana sits between an AI agent and the business systems it acts on. It decides whether each action is allowed, and it keeps a record.

Read the earlier parts in order if you are deciding whether to use it: what it does, how to choose, how to run it, and how to check the evidence.

## What it does not do

Parmana does not replace identity management, your own audit, or your legal review. It is one control in your stack.
