Opening: two to four sentences. State the problem, who it affects, and what this post covers.

## What it does

Explain the capability in plain terms. Link to its code or doc, for example [the gateway](https://docs.parmanasystems.com/concepts/the-gateway).

## What it does not do

State the limits plainly. This section is required.

## How we checked it

Describe the test, scenario or run, and link to the evidence. Show real output where you show a command.

## What is still open

List known gaps, and what would close each one.
